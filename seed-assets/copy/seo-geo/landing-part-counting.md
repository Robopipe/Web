## What the camera counts

Part counting with a camera fits wherever a pack must hold an exact number of pieces. Examples from production sites:

- **Capsules in a container.** Every container must leave with the right number of capsules.
- **Pieces in a pack.** The camera counts the pieces in every pack that passes under it.
- **Fill level.** The camera sees when a container or a blister is not full.

Operators see piece counts, the share of OK pieces and the line rate on a display on the floor.

## Counting app or camera on the line

A part counting app on a phone counts one pile when somebody takes a photo. That is not enough on a line, where packs come one after another. Robopipe is a fixed camera over the belt. It counts every pack without the operator and sends the result on.

The model learns counting from images of your products. This is computer vision. The [guide to machine vision](/en/blog/what-is-a-vision-system-a-plain-english-guide-to-machine-vision-in-manufacturing) tells you more about the field.

## A record for every pack

Robopipe saves an image of every check. When a customer or an auditor asks about a batch, you can find the image. You can export the image archive of OK and NOK pieces for an audit.
