## What Robopipe does

Robopipe delivers AI quality inspection for industrial sites. It is AI quality inspection that learns your product by itself. A sealed camera over the line checks every piece and a deep-learning model decides whether the piece matches the sample. The [camera quality inspection](/en/camera-quality-inspection) page shows how it works.

Customers set up new products themselves in the Robopipe Studio app. In the Pro and Enterprise plans, [auto annotation](/en/auto-annotation) helps: AI suggests boxes on the images, and a person reviews and approves the suggestions.

## Who we work for

## Open source

The base of the platform is open. The [robopipe organisation on GitHub](https://github.com/robopipe) describes Robopipe as an open source machine vision platform. You can run the open-source version yourself on your own infrastructure.
