## What machine vision is

Machine vision is the industrial use of [computer vision](/en/computer-vision). A camera on the line captures the products. An inference unit decides on every piece and the verdict controls machines: a reject mechanism, a signal beacon or a downstream PLC.

## Integration on the line

- **Mounted over your existing belt.** The line gets no extra mechanical step and does not slow down.
- **Triggered capture.** The camera captures on an encoder pulse, a light barrier or a fixed frame rate.
- **Industrial interfaces.** The AI PLC controller has analogue and digital I/O, RS485/RS232, Modbus and EtherCAT over Ethernet.
- **Results into your systems.** Verdicts switch physical outputs and are published over Modbus or EtherCAT. On the Enterprise plan we also connect Robopipe to your ERP and MES.
- **Visibility for people.** Operators see results on the floor display. Managers follow trends, downtime and batch comparisons in the analytics portal.

## Compared with classic machine vision systems

Classic machine vision systems are set up with rules. Every new product or change in appearance means new rules. Robopipe takes a different approach:

- **Learning instead of rules.** The model learns from images of your products. You add a new product yourself in minutes. The [AI quality inspection](/en/ai-quality-inspection) page has the details.
- **No integrator.** We mount the camera and commission it on site. Most lines start inspecting within a day of installation.
- **Hardware included.** The camera and the AI PLC controller come with every paid plan as a rental. You pay a fixed monthly price per camera.
- **An open system.** You can read the configurations, export the datasets and take your models with you. No black box stands between you and your quality decisions.
