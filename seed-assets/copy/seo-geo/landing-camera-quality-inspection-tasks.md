## Visual quality inspection by task

A camera system for production inspection solves a different task on every site. Optical quality inspection from Robopipe always uses the same industrial AI camera and the same rollout. Automated visual inspection of products differs only in what the model looks for in the image.

[Defect detection on the production line](/en/defect-detection): the camera finds a surface defect, a shape error or a foreign object in the pack.

[Part counting with a camera](/en/camera-part-counting): the camera counts the pieces in every pack and saves an image of every check.

[Label inspection](/en/label-inspection): the camera checks that the label is present and in position and that the batch code is legible, and reads the best-before date.

[Packaging completeness inspection](/en/packaging-completeness-inspection): the camera checks that nothing is missing from the pack and nothing extra is in it.
