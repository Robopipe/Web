## What camera quality inspection is

Camera quality inspection means a camera and an inference unit judge each product on the line instead of a person. The camera captures every piece. A model checks whether the piece matches the reference. The verdict then goes to the operator display, to the reject mechanism or to your systems.

Spot-checking every tenth piece keeps the line at speed, but defects slip through by the batch. Checking every piece by hand means more people on the line. A camera does not have to make that trade-off. At 3,600 pieces per hour it judges each product within 50 milliseconds.

## Hardware built for the factory floor

- **IP67 camera.** It is fully sealed, so it handles steam, pressure washing, dust and freezer cold. You wash it down with the rest of the floor during sanitation.
- **Images in line with the line.** The 12 MP camera captures on an encoder pulse, a light barrier or a fixed frame rate.
- **Inference at the line.** The AI PLC controller decides on every piece. It has analogue and digital I/O, RS485/RS232, Modbus and EtherCAT over Ethernet.
- **Connected to your machines.** The verdict fires your existing reject mechanism or signal beacon. The line does not slow down and gets no extra mechanical step.
